## Hook templates

Hooks lead with the **result or the stakes**, then the how. List the creator's proven hook
shapes with a real example of each:

- **"[template with {blanks}]"** → *[real example]*
- **"[template]"** → *[real example]*

*Common high-performers to consider: "i [made/built/shipped] {thing} as a {relatable
identity}", "{thing} and it actually works", "{time ago} i had ZERO idea how to {do thing}",
listicle hooks ("{X} {things} {outcome}"), milestone hooks, empathy hooks ("if you {want
outcome} but feel overwhelmed about where to start").*

Avoid hooks that are vague, clever-for-clever's-sake, or that bury the result. No "let me
tell you a story about..." windups. Result first.
